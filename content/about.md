# About Me

Welcome to my personal website! I'm Boxi Xia, a postdoctoral researcher at Duke University working with Prof. Boyuan Chen in the General Robotics Lab.

## Research

My research focuses on robot hardware design and control, with particular emphasis on legged locomotion systems including humanoids and soft robots. I develop learning-based control algorithms using reinforcement learning and build physics-based simulators to enable efficient robot design and control. My work spans the full pipeline from mechanical design and fabrication to simulation, learning, and real-world deployment.

Recent projects include designing the Duke Humanoid, a 10-DOF child-sized bipedal robot for energy-efficient locomotion using passive dynamics, and developing Text2Robot, a framework that uses generative AI to automatically design and manufacture quadrupedal robots from text descriptions.

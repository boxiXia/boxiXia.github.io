Boxi Xia

Postdoctoral Associate, Duke University

I'm working toward robots that can carry out long, precise physical tasks reliably. I design and build the hardware, train the control in simulation, and run it on real robots. The projects below are the pieces I've built so far. Next is putting them together into robots that run multi-step tasks from start to finish.

I work in the General Robotics Lab at Duke with Prof. Boyuan Chen. I did my Ph.D. in the Creative Machines Lab at Columbia with Prof. Hod Lipson, and before Duke I built learned locomotion controllers for quadrupeds at PX Robotics.
